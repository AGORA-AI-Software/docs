## Reporting a Vulnerability

To report a security vulnerability, email [security@agoraai.tech](mailto:security@agoraai.tech).

Please include:

- A description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce
- Potential impact
- Any relevant screenshots or proof-of-concept

We will acknowledge your report within 48 hours and aim to resolve confirmed vulnerabilities within 90 days.
